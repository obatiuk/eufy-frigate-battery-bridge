# `AT-10`: Hardware Power Manager Sync & Stuck Spotlight (`light`) Conservation

- Hardware Power Manager sync and experimental stuck-spotlight (`light`) conservation:
  1. When `sync_power: true` (default), all `device.set_property` commands for a camera must execute sequentially (never
     concurrently in parallel) to sync `powerWorkingMode`, `recordingClipLength`, `recordingEndClipMotionStops`,
     `recordingRetriggerInterval`, and clamp non-doorbell `videoStreamingQuality` to Medium (`7`).
  2. The bridge must never turn on or toggle the camera's built-in spotlight (`light`) during normal session start/stop
     or at startup. Experimental stuck-spotlight tracking (`track_stuck_spotlight`) must default to `false`, leaving
     `light` untouched even if `light: true` stays reported past `130s` while `IDLE`.
  3. Only when `track_stuck_spotlight: true` is configured (globally or per-camera), the bridge turns off a stuck
     spotlight (`light: false`) via the `500ms` housekeeping watchdog (or idle wedged-radio recovery) whenever the
     camera is `IDLE`, has
     no active hardware triggers (`state: false`), no pending `BackfillJob`, no backfill playback, and `light: true` has
     stayed on for `> 130s` since the later of the light turning on and the last session or motion activity; a freshly
     lit spotlight is never judged stuck (it may be lighting a hardware clip that is still recording).
