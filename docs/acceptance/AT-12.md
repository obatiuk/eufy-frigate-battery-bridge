# `AT-12`: `end_early: false` Mid-Session Re-Wake, `end_early: true` Clean End, Hard Duration Caps & `idle_timeout_secs`

- During an active live session:
  1. When `end_early: false` (default in `"rtsp"`, `"p2p"`, `"auto"`), if the camera hardware cuts off the `RTSP` or
     `P2P` stream before `hold_secs` has elapsed since `lastMotionAt` (or while Eufy hardware triggers, Frigate object
     detections, or human viewers are still active), the bridge must immediately re-wake the stream to finish the hold
     window; once `hold_secs` has elapsed and triggers/objects have cleared, a hardware stream stop must sleep cleanly.
  2. When `end_early: true` (default in `"backfill"`, or optional override), once all active Eufy triggers report
     `state: false` and at least `10s` of video was captured, the bridge shortens the remaining hold to `5s` and treats
     subsequent hardware stream stops as a clean event end.
  3. Continuous motion/event sessions must be hard-capped at `stream_max_secs` (`900s` default) from `startedAt`, and
     viewer-only sessions (`!isInMotion(now)`) must be hard-capped at `view_max_secs` (`300s` default) from
     `Math.max(startedAt, motionUntil)`, forcing a clean battery-conservation sleep when reached.
  4. When `idle_timeout_secs > 0` and no human viewer hold is active (`now >= viewUntil`), an active motion session that
     has streamed for at least `idle_timeout_secs` since `firstVideoAt` must sleep early (`evaluateEarlySleep`) once
     either (a) all Eufy hardware triggers report `state: false` for `>= 5s` and Frigate reports zero active tracked
     objects (`eufy-cleared`), or (b) `idle_timeout_secs` has elapsed since `lastMotionAt` and Frigate reports zero
     active tracked objects (`quiet-timeout`).
  5. On a camera configured with an explicit `rtsp_url` (direct `RTSP`), all session duration, hold expiry, viewer
     linger, viewer cooldown, Frigate hold-extension poll, and `idle_timeout_secs` early-sleep timeouts must be bypassed
     (`hasExpiredLiveHold() === false`, `hasReachedMaxDuration() === false`, `hasReachedViewMaxDuration() === false`,
     `canSleepEarlyOnIdle() === false`, `checkEarlySleepDue().due === false`,
     `shouldPollFrigateForHoldOrBackfill().shouldPoll === false`, `isInViewerCooldown() === false`) so an active direct
     `RTSP` stream flows continuously until the `RTSP` stream itself ends or drops.
