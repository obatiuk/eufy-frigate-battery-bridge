# `AT-12`: Default `end_early: true` Clean End, `RTSP` Clip Continuation, `end_early: false` Re-Wake & Session Caps

- During an active live session:
  1. `end_early` defaults to `true` across all Eufy modes (with base `hold_secs` defaulting to `30s` in `"auto"`,
     `"rtsp"`, `"p2p"`, and `"custom"` and `20s` in `"backfill"` or `backfill_only: true`). Once all active Eufy
     triggers report `state: false` and at least `10s` of video was captured, the bridge shortens remaining
     `motionUntil` to `5s` and treats hardware stream stops as a clean event end when no waking human viewer, Eufy
     trigger, or Frigate tracked object remains active; while a waking human viewer (`hasWakingViewer()`) or active
     Eufy/Frigate detection remains active within duration caps, `RTSP` clip-boundary drops must immediately re-wake to
     continue the session.
  2. When `end_early: false` is explicitly configured, if the camera hardware cuts off the `RTSP` or `P2P` stream before
     `hold_secs` has elapsed since `lastMotionAt` (or while Eufy hardware triggers, Frigate object detections, or human
     viewers are still active), the bridge must immediately re-wake the stream to finish the hold window; once
     `hold_secs` has elapsed and triggers/objects have cleared, a hardware stream stop must sleep cleanly.
  3. Continuous **live** motion/event sessions (`isActiveLiveSession()`) must be hard-capped at `stream_max_secs`
     (`900s` default) from `startedAt`, and live viewer-only sessions (`!isInMotion(now)`) must be hard-capped at
     `view_max_secs` (`300s` default) from `Math.max(startedAt, motionUntil)`, forcing a clean battery-conservation
     sleep when reached; neither `stream_max_secs` nor `view_max_secs` may truncate `STREAM_MODES.DOWNLOAD` HomeBase
     clip backfill playback (`hasReachedMaxDuration() === false`, `hasReachedViewMaxDuration() === false`).
  4. When `idle_timeout_secs > 0` and no human viewer hold is active (`now >= viewUntil`), an active motion session that
     has streamed for at least `idle_timeout_secs` since `firstVideoAt` must sleep early (`evaluateEarlySleep`) once
     either (a) all Eufy hardware triggers report `state: false` for `>= 5s` and Frigate reports zero active tracked
     objects (`eufy-cleared`), or (b) `idle_timeout_secs` has elapsed since `lastMotionAt` and Frigate reports zero
     active tracked objects (`quiet-timeout`).
  5. On a camera configured with an explicit `rtsp_url` (direct `RTSP`), all session duration, hold expiry, viewer
     linger, viewer cooldown, Frigate hold-extension poll, and `idle_timeout_secs` early-sleep timeouts must be bypassed
     (`hasExpiredLiveHold() === false`, `hasReachedMaxDuration() === false`, `hasReachedViewMaxDuration() === false`,
     `canSleepEarlyOnIdle() === false`, `checkEarlySleepDue().due === false`,
     `shouldPollFrigateForHoldOrBackfill().shouldPoll === false`, `isInViewerCooldown() === false`) so an active direct
     `RTSP` stream flows continuously until the `RTSP` stream itself ends or drops.
