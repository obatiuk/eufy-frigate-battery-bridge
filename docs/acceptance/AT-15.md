# `AT-15`: Proportional `20%` Safety-Net Backfill Cutoff & Bounded `120s` Recording Wait

- For HomeBase 3 `BackfillJob` scheduling:
  1. In live modes (`"auto"`, `"rtsp"`, `"p2p"` with `backfill_enabled: true`), the safety-net `BackfillJob` is
     canceled once the live stream decodes **`20%` of the camera's configured clip/hold duration (clamped to `3s..15s`,
     e.g., `4s` for `20s`, `6s` for `30s`, `12s` for `60s`)** unless the session is later preempted mid-event (`AT-13`).
  2. `BackfillJob` must wait for all active trigger properties on that camera to report `state: false` + `4s` settle
     (and `2s` station `P2P` slot idle via `backfill_delay_secs`), with a fallback of `max(35s, hold_secs + 4s)` after
     the latest trigger if `state: false` is lost, hard-capped at Eufy's `120s` maximum hardware clip length (`+ 4s`
     settle = `124s`) from the initial trigger (`this.at`), and expiring stale jobs older than `300s` (`5 minutes`).
     Un-cleared camera hardware triggers expire after `130s` (or immediately on station/`WebSocket` disconnect or device
     offline), and once a pending `BackfillJob` has settled or matched its clip, a new `state: true` trigger on that
     camera must keep the settled job queryable and queue a separate follow-on `BackfillJob` instead of regressing the
     settled job back to `RECORDING`.
