# `AT-14`: Human Frigate Live View - Dashboard Grid Ignore, Same-Station Switching & Blocks

- Across all camera modes in `pollViewers`:
  1. Simultaneous new viewer connections across multiple cameras (`isDashboardGrid`) must be ignored so opening the
     Frigate multi-camera dashboard grid never wakes sleeping cameras.
  2. Opening a single camera's Live View on a HomeBase must **only** stop another Live View camera on the same HomeBase
     if **both** cameras are `P2P` cameras (`!isRtspCapable()`) competing for the HomeBase's single `P2P` slot and the
     other camera is not playing out a backfill clip (`!isBackfillPlayback()`); `RTSP` Live View cameras and active
     backfill playbacks must never be stopped by a viewer switch.
  3. Single-camera Live View wakes must be blocked when the camera is (a) offline, (b) in Low-Battery Economy Mode with
     `low_battery_wake_on_view: false`, (c) within `view_cooldown_secs` (`5s`) after sleeping, (d) a `P2P` camera
     whose HomeBase `P2P` slot is busy with an active `P2P` motion event, or (e) `IDLE` with a pending `BackfillJob`
     (`hasPendingBackfill()`, logging `ignored, waiting for HomeBase clip backfill` and keeping the **Yellow**
     `Waiting` / `busy` status card visible).
