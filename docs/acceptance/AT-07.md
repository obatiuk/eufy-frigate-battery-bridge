# `AT-07`: Offline Detection, Deep-Standby Protection & Battery-Swap Recovery

- Across all camera modes:
  1. A healthy battery camera in deep standby (`state: 0` with `battery > 5%` and `wifiRssi > -95 dBm`) **must never**
     be marked offline.
  2. A camera reporting `state: 3` (`OFFLINE_LOWBAT`), `state: 2/4/5`, `0%` unplugged battery, `state: 0` with critical
     battery (`<= 5%`) or dead Wi-Fi (`<= -95 dBm`), or an unresponsive non-event wake timeout (with zero device events
     received during the session) must be marked offline, stopping its FFmpeg `RTSP` encoder (after seeding the cached
     snapshot for `6s` when `sleep_screen: "snapshot"`).
  3. When a depleted battery is swapped for a charged battery (`>= 10%` jump or `battery > low_battery_threshold`), the
     camera must clear its offline state even if `device.get_properties` still carries a stale `state: 3`, and
     automatically restart its FFmpeg `RTSP` encoder.
  4. Online cameras in Low-Battery Economy Mode (`<= low_battery_threshold`, default `20%` unplugged) shorten live event
     holds to `low_battery_hold_secs` (`20s`) and must never trigger `driver.poll_refresh` on background polls or viewer
     connections.
