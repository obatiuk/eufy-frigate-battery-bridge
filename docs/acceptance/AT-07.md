# `AT-07`: Offline Detection, Deep-Standby Protection & Battery-Swap Recovery

- Across all camera modes:
  1. A healthy battery camera in deep standby (`state: 0` with `battery > 5%` and `wifiRssi > -95 dBm`) **must never**
     be marked offline.
  2. A camera reporting `state: 3` (`OFFLINE_LOWBAT`), `state: 2/4/5` (`MANUALLY_DISABLED` / `REMOVE_AND_READD` /
     `RESET_AND_READD`), `0%` unplugged battery, `state: 0` with critical battery (`<= 5%`) or dead Wi-Fi
     (`<= -95 dBm`), or an unresponsive non-event wake timeout (with zero device events received during the session)
     must be marked offline, stopping its FFmpeg `RTSP` encoder (after seeding the cached snapshot for `6s` when
     `sleep_screen: "snapshot"`).
  3. When a depleted battery is swapped for a charged battery (`>= 10%` jump or `battery > low_battery_threshold`), the
     camera must clear its offline state even if `device.get_properties` still carries a stale `state: 3`, and
     automatically restart its FFmpeg `RTSP` encoder.
  4. Online cameras in Low-Battery Economy Mode (`<= low_battery_threshold`, default `30%` unplugged) shorten live event
     holds to `low_battery_hold_secs` (`20s`) and, together with manually disabled or unpaired cameras (`state: 2/4/5`),
     **must never** cause background `driver.poll_refresh` calls; unforced background `driver.poll_refresh` for genuine
     dead-battery, dead-link, or wake-timeout offline cameras must respect a `60`-minute (`3600s`) cooldown while
     explicit forced refreshes remain immediate.
