# `AT-02`: `"backfill"` Mode on `P2P`-Only Cameras & Doorbells - Motion, `/api/wake` & Hardware Events While `IDLE`

- When a non-RTSP camera or doorbell configured with `mode: "backfill"` is `IDLE`:
  1. Eufy motion, person, or doorbell ring events (`state: true`) **must never** wake a live `P2P` or `RTSP` session.
     The camera must remain `IDLE`, queue a `BackfillJob`, and display the **Yellow** (`Waiting` / `busy`) status
     indicator until backfill playback starts or the job expires. If `POST /api/wake/:cameras` arrives while an
     unexpired Eufy hardware trigger (`<= 130s`, cleared on station/`WebSocket` disconnect or device offline) or
     `BackfillJob` is active, it must also stay `IDLE` and upgrade/extend the `BackfillJob` (`status: "backfill"`).
  2. When `'rtsp livestream started'` or a trigger-paired `'livestream started'` (while a motion/AI/ring trigger or
     `BackfillJob` is active) is emitted by `eufy-security-ws` while `IDLE`, it **must never** start a live session;
     instead, it must queue or extend the `BackfillJob` time window (and mark that trigger cleared on
     `'livestream stopped'` / `'rtsp livestream stopped'`) so HomeBase 3 is queried and any recorded `.zxvideo` clip is
     replayed via backfill.
  3. When a standalone `'livestream started'` event arrives while `IDLE` outside the `3s` post-sleep guard with **no**
     active Eufy motion/AI/ring trigger or pending `BackfillJob` (e.g., a human opened Live View in the Eufy mobile
     app), the bridge **must** piggyback on the already-flowing `P2P` stream (`p2pReady: true`, without sending
     `device.start_livestream`) and stop immediately when `'livestream stopped'` arrives.
