# `AT-16`: Backfill `15 fps` Frame-0 Playback, Multi-Clip Continuity, Live `P2P` Isolation & Download Retry

- During HomeBase 3 `.zxvideo` backfill download and playback:
  1. Multiple matching clips for an event must be queued in chronological order (`oldest -> newest`), upgrading label
     and recognized `personName` in-place on companion triggers, and scheduling a follow-up query when activity extends
     `>= 15s` past the clip start (or querying `station.database_query_latest_info` in the background during
     `DRAINING_BACKFILL_TAIL`).
  2. Playback must start from **Frame 0** (`FB` -> `B`) at `15 fps` with synchronized audio, pause decoder `stdout` at
     `30` buffered frames and resume at `10` frames to bound RAM, flush all remaining frames at `EOF`, and hold the
     final frame for `2.5s` (`0s` hold between back-to-back clips without flashing the `"Asleep"` card).
  3. While in `STREAM_MODES.DOWNLOAD` (`isBackfillPlayback()`), the bridge must ignore incoming live `P2P` events
     (`'livestream started'`, `'livestream stopped'`, `'livestream video data'`, and `'livestream audio data'`) so
     opening or closing the Eufy mobile app never corrupts or stops the backfill playback, must let the current clip
     finish playing uninterrupted while queuing a `BackfillJob` in the background if new Eufy motion,
     `'rtsp livestream started'`, or `POST /api/wake/:cameras` (`status: "backfill"`) arrives on a `"backfill"` camera,
     and if `device.start_download` fails or times out before decoding Frame 0 (`!gotVideo`), or the transfer is
     interrupted after Frame 0 (station disconnect, `WebSocket` loss, preemption by a live event,
     `device.cancel_download`) before `'download finished'` arrived or playback was ended as complete
     (`eufy/download-finished`), must remove `clip.storagePath` from `downloadedPaths`, re-queue the clip at the front
     of `backfillClipQueue` (preserving `pendingBackfill`), and retry from Frame 0 after a `4s` cooldown up to `3`
     download attempts in total (`MAX_BACKFILL_CLIP_DOWNLOAD_ATTEMPTS = 3`). A finished transfer, or playback ended
     as complete after the `3s` idle fallback, stays downloaded and is never re-queued.
