# `AT-16`: Backfill `15 fps` Frame-0 Playback, Multi-Clip Continuity, Live `P2P` Isolation & Download Retry

- During HomeBase 3 `.zxvideo` backfill download and playback:
  1. Normal (non-`SIGUSR1`-test) `BackfillJob`s must reject timestamp-less database candidates (`clipMs <= 0`), and
     station-wide database query results must ignore cameras whose own hardware recording is still in progress before
     settle/timeout. Multiple matching clips for an event must be queued in chronological order (`oldest -> newest`),
     upgrading label and recognized `personName` in-place on companion triggers, and scheduling a follow-up query
     (resetting the `10`-attempt query budget across `3+` split clips without adding a redundant `4s` settle delay) when
     activity extends `>= 15s` past the clip start (or querying `station.database_query_latest_info` in the background
     during `DRAINING_BACKFILL_TAIL`).
  2. Playback must start from **Frame 0** (`FB` -> `B`) at `15 fps` with synchronized audio (playing full `90s`-`120s`
     clips up to `MAX_DOWNLOAD_BUFFER_SECS = 120` to completion without being cut off by `stream_max_secs` or
     `view_max_secs`), pause decoder `stdout` at `30` buffered frames and resume at `10` frames to bound RAM, flush all
     remaining frames at `EOF` (deferring decoder detach until `stdout` closes so flowing tail frames are never
     dropped), and hold the final frame for `2.5s` (`0s` hold across queued clips and pending follow-up or back-to-back
     `BackfillJob` continuations without flashing the `"Asleep"` or `"Waiting"` card).
  3. While in `STREAM_MODES.DOWNLOAD` (`isBackfillPlayback()`), the bridge must ignore incoming live `P2P` events
     (`'livestream started'`, `'livestream stopped'`, `'livestream video data'`, and `'livestream audio data'`) so
     opening or closing the Eufy mobile app never corrupts or stops the backfill playback, must **never** self-preempt
     the active clip playback on the same camera (preserving strict chronological event order: queuing a `BackfillJob`
     in the background if new Eufy motion/AI/ring or `'rtsp livestream started'` arrives with `backfill_enabled: true`
     or if `POST /api/wake/:cameras` arrives on a `"backfill"` camera or while an unexpired Eufy hardware trigger is
     recording with `status: "backfill"`, and otherwise deferring same-camera live wakes via `status: "scheduled"` so
     the full requested live hold starts immediately after backfill playback finishes), and if `device.start_download`
     fails or times out (`15s`) before decoding Frame 0 (`firstFrameAt > 0`, even if raw compressed packets or
     `'download finished'` arrived), or the transfer is interrupted after Frame 0 (station disconnect, `WebSocket` loss,
     preemption by another camera's live event, `device.cancel_download`) before `'download finished'` arrived or
     playback was ended as complete (`eufy/download-finished`), must remove `clip.storagePath` from `downloadedPaths`,
     re-queue the clip at the front of `backfillClipQueue` (preserving the active and any queued back-to-back
     `BackfillJob`), and retry from Frame 0 after a `4s` cooldown up to `3` download attempts in total
     (`MAX_BACKFILL_CLIP_DOWNLOAD_ATTEMPTS = 3`). Once Frame 0 has decoded (`firstFrameAt > 0`) and decoder pipes have
     drained, a finished transfer (`'download finished'`) or playback ended as complete via the `3s` idle fallback stays
     downloaded and is never re-queued.
