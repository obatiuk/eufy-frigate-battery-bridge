# `AT-09`: Post-Viewer Linger Clean Termination & Trigger-Bypassed Viewer Cooldown

- In all camera modes, when a human viewer closes Frigate Live View (`hasWakingViewer() === false`) and the camera is in
  its post-viewer linger window (`view_linger_secs`), if the camera hardware stops sending `RTSP` or `P2P` video, the
  bridge must terminate the session cleanly (`'expired'`) and **must never** re-wake the camera in a loop.
- The post-viewer cooldown (`view_cooldown_secs`) only guards pure viewer re-wakes and is bypassed whenever a Eufy
  hardware trigger is active.
